FACE PUNCH - build plan (plain text, kept short)

ART DIRECTION: "Saturday night toy prizefight"
One look, used everywhere: a dark arena, one bright boxing ring, glossy toy-like
shapes, comic lettering. You see the fight from the challenger's eyes, like
Punch-Out and Wii Sports Boxing: the opponent's big head fills the middle, your
two cartoon gloves sit at the bottom of the screen.
- Palette: night navy and purple crowd, a blue ring mat under a warm spotlight,
  magenta and cyan rim lights, yellow for every number and banner, red corner
  against blue corner.
- The head wears padded sparring headgear in its corner colour. It hides the
  seam between the scanned face and the back of the head, so the head can turn
  and wobble without looking like a flat mask. Neck, shoulders and body under it.
- Lettering: Bangers (comic display font from jsDelivr) for banners and numbers,
  system font for small text. Big, high contrast, readable on a phone.

GAME FEEL (from shipped fighters and common practice)
- Hit-stop: 40 to 60 ms for light hits, 90 to 140 ms for heavy, 250 ms on the KO.
- Camera: directional kick, quick push-in that springs back, shake that grows
  with the square of "trauma" and includes a little roll.
- Squash and stretch: glove stretches in flight and squashes on contact, the
  head squashes then the jelly springs carry on.
- Layered hit: freeze, flash, shockwave ring, comic burst, sparks, a comic word,
  thump. Small hits get small effects so the big ones stand out.
- KO: slow motion, zoom, dark red backdrop with speed lines, "K.O." slams in,
  stars circle the head, then the head springs upright: "BOUNCE BACK!".
- Health bar with a white trail that lags behind the damage.

THE THREE MOMENTS EVERYTHING SERVES
1. Scan becomes a 3D head (scan-line reveal with a wire mesh, slow turn to show
   depth, live expressions when a camera is on).
2. The camera reads your hands (gloves follow them, punches detected).
3. The impact (jelly, swelling lumps, bobble-head neck, the effects above).

MODES
- Quick play: cartoon head, no camera, no download. Starts in two seconds.
- My face: scan with the camera or pick a photo. Faces never leave the device.
- Solo: 30 second rush. Every KO the head bounces back tougher. Score and rank.
- Two players: both pick a face, take turns punching the other one's head.
  First health bar at zero loses. Rematch button.

FILES
index.html, style.css  page, HUD and screens
main.js       boot and the frame loop
bus.js        tiny event bus (the punch event travels on it)
arena.js      ring, crowd, lights, camera rig
head.js       scan to mesh, jelly physics, bruises, cartoon head
fighter.js    body, neck, sway, fall and revive
gloves.js     the player's gloves
hands.js      hand tracking results to punch events
tracking.js   loads the tracking library and data, runs the camera
input.js      tap, drag and key punches
game.js       rules: health, KO, rounds, turns, score
fx.js         hit-stop, slow motion, particles, rings, comic words
sound.js      fx.sound(name): files from assets/audio if present, else synth
ui.js         screens, HUD, banners, loading

ONE EVENT: every input emits  punch {x, y, dx, dy, strength, source}
in head-plane units (face width = 2, strength 0.35 to 1.8).

ORDER OF WORK
1. Split the prototype into modules, photo punching works again.   (first)
2. Arena, lights, headgear and body, gloves.
3. Effects, KO and revive, HUD.
4. Cartoon head, solo rush, two player turns.
5. Camera path, loading, quality switch, sound hooks.
6. Test, look at screenshots, fix, measure frame rate.
